## What each one says

- **Infosys (29, Buy):** the cheapest name on the list, at a P/E of 14 against 19 for IT, with very high quality (ROE 31%). The price is 45% below its 5-year high and has fallen about 9% a year for 3 and 5 years while EPS still grew about 10% a year, so the multiple has de-rated hard. The board flags that an AI-driven sector de-rating may be structural. Momentum is weak (74).
- **DCB Bank (32, Buy):** a P/E of 9 is about half the bank sector's. The price is up 29% over the year and at its 5-year high, which is why momentum is favourable (22). Quality is middling: 5-year ROE is only 11%. The scores rest on 4 years of EPS data.
- **ITC (37, Accumulate):** a P/E of 20 against 33 for FMCG, with strong quality (ROE 32%). It is 40% off its 5-year high and has been range-bound for 8½ months. The FY25 EPS includes the ITC Hotels demerger gain. Momentum is weak (72).
- **ICICI Bank (40, Accumulate):** the best compounder here, with EPS up 23% a year and the price up about 16–20% a year for 5–10 years. It trades at a premium to the sector (P/E 20 vs 17), so the valuation bucket is the weak spot (57). It sits near its high, with no value setup.
- **HDFC Bank (43, Accumulate):** a P/E of 16, slightly below the sector, with EPS growing 11% a year. The price has compounded only about 2% a year for 5 years and has consolidated for 8 months, so the multiple has compressed. Momentum is weak (64).
- **HUL (43, Accumulate):** excellent quality (9) but expensive at a P/E of 43 against 33 for FMCG. The price is below where it was 5 years ago and 33% off its high. FY26 EPS includes a one-off from the ice-cream demerger, so the latest ROE is inflated.
- **Amara Raja (43, Accumulate):** cheap on P/E (17 against 31 for auto) but with weak quality (ROE 12%) and poor momentum (75). It's the only watchlist stock in a **short-term drawdown** (−21% from its 3-month high), and it's 57% below its 5-year high. Cheap, but falling.
- **Dixon (47, Accumulate):** the growth story, with EPS up 77% a year and ROE 27%, at a P/E of 46 against 39 for the sector. Momentum is weak (76) after an 11% fall from its 3-month high. The price is still up about 21% a year over 5 years.
- **Castrol India (48, Accumulate):** the board's "quality at a price" flag: the best ROE on the list (44%) but a P/E of 19 against a sector at 13, with EPS growing only 5% a year. It has consolidated for 15 months and is 31% off its 5-year high.
- **Gulf Oil Lubricants (48, Accumulate):** similar business to Castrol but cheaper (P/E 14) and growing faster (EPS +14% a year). The price is up about 15% a year over 5 years. Valuation sits mid-range (55).
- **Symphony (not rated):** ROE is −20% this year, so there is no usable P/E and the model can't score valuation. The price is 71% below its 5-year high and has fallen 12% a year for 5 years. Wait for earnings to recover before treating it as value.
