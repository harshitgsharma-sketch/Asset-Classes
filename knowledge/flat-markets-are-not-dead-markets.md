# Flat markets are not dead markets: sectors keep rotating

- **Source:** [How Sector Rotation Helps You in Any Market Condition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=90xkdiThhcY), WeekendInvesting (YouTube), presented by Alok Jain. 11 minutes, English. Published 24 Dec 2025.

## Main point

A flat headline index hides a lot of movement underneath. Even when the Nifty makes no new high for years, some sectors are in strong bull runs, and the leaders change from one flat phase to the next.

## The evidence he shows

**Nifty has had long flat phases** (no new all-time high), lasting 2 to 6 years: 2000–04, 2008–14, 2019–21, and one running since mid-2024. Midcaps had 2001–03, 2008–14, 2018–21 and the last year; small caps went almost 9–10 years (2008–17) without a new high, then another 3 years.

**Six sector indices through the two flat phases** (2008–14 and 2018–21). These six make up about 42% of the Nifty 500 and have long data histories:

| Sector | 2008–14 flat phase | 2018–21 flat phase |
|---|---|---|
| Auto | Strong bull run (yearly returns of 19–66%) | No returns |
| Pharma | Strong (30–46% a year) | Little until late |
| FMCG | Strong (36%, 61%) | Strong (32%, 35%) |
| IT | Strong (24–30%) | Even stronger (33–54%) |
| Bank | Two good years | Three good years out of four |
| Realty | Nothing; no new high since 2008 | Nothing |

**Takeaways**

- The leaders of one flat phase (autos, pharma) became laggards in the next, and earlier laggards (IT, banks) took over.
- Defensives (FMCG, IT, pharma) did well in both flat phases.
- In calendar-year sector tables, no sector stays at the top for long. It is a relay race.
- The same is true across asset classes: while Indian equity rests, gold and silver run; two years earlier it was real estate.

## His prescription

Don't try to predict the next leader. Use a rule-based ("sector agnostic") momentum approach that follows price strength and rotates automatically. And don't quit equities during flat phases: retail investors historically give up just before the next bull run.
